# rotation_scores

Scores of every crop rotation of 3 crops (125, order matters) for field `ks-kearny-01`, each without and (where it fits)
with a cover crop, ranked with weights,
plus detail data for the best ones. Generated by `crop_scorer/scoring/score_rotations.py`
(`uv run python -m crop_scorer.scoring.score_rotations`, `--rescore` recomputes all rotations, ~1.5 min).

## Method in short

- **Same days for all scores:** seasons from `water_usage.build_timeline` (sowing in the middle of the NASS
  most-active window, or 3 days after the previous harvest inside the full window). Window = first sowing to
  last harvest of one pass through the rotation, replayed for every start year that fits into 1991–2025 (~33).
- **Cover crop variants** (`rotation` ends in `+triticale`, columns `crops` and `cover`): triticale in every gap between
  two crops of the pass where it fits (not after the last crop). Sown 3 days after harvest inside the winter wheat
  NASS planting window (Sep 10 - Nov 1), terminated 35 days before the next sowing (NRCS termination guidelines,
  non-irrigated zone 1), at least 28 days (assumption). Grows with the winter wheat Kc curve (proxy, assumption) from
  the soil water bucket (rain only, not irrigated), so the next crop starts drier. Cover days are not bare. The crop
  after it needs 27.5 lb N/ac more (SARE, Managing Cover Crops Profitably p. 46, 25-30 lb after a cereal cover).
  100 of the 125 rotations have a gap that fits, so there are 225 candidates.
- **Raw scores per day**, mean over the start years (smaller is better):
  - `water_mm_per_day`: irrigation need (ETc − ETa, see `water_usage`) summed over the window / window days
  - `risk_per_crop_day`: mean daily temperature risk score over crop days (see `crop_scorer/risk_score`)
  - `bare_share`: days without main crop or cover crop / window days
  - `n_fertilizer_kg_per_day`: N fertilizer need after K-State MF2586 (kg N/ha) summed over the seasons / window days
    (`crop_scorer/scoring/nitrogen.py`). Expected yield = NASS practice "all" (1.05 x mean, `expected_yield_clean.csv`),
    previous crop = the crop before it in the rotation, none for the first season. Soybean: 0 (no K-State formula,
    legume, assumption). Corn uses the irrigated K-State factors (county yield is close to the irrigated level).
    Does not depend on the weather.
- **Normalised scores** `water_score`, `risk_score`, `soil_health_score`, `nitrogen_score`: min-max over all 225
  candidates, 0 = best, 1 = worst.
- **Ranking:** `total_score` = weighted mean of the four `*_score`. Weights and the farmer's rotation come from
  `crop_scorer/scoring/ui_input_mock.json` (MOCK until the UI exists; the farmer's rotation is a random draw).

## Files

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `rotation_scores.csv` | all 125 rotations: raw and normalised scores (cache for re-ranking, no weights) |
| `rotation_seasons_by_year.csv` | all 125 rotations: every season in every weather year (for spreads, yield, any rotation a user picks) |
| `rotation_monthly.csv` | all 125 rotations: calendar months of the window, mean over the weather years |
| `top_rotations.csv` | top 10 by `total_score` + the farmer's rotation (`is_current`), with `rank` among all |
| `top_rotations_seasons.csv` | the seasons of these rotations, with per-season values |
| `top_rotations_daily_long.csv` | daily values of these rotations, mean over the start years |

`rotation_scores.csv` / `top_rotations.csv`: `rotation` (crops joined by `-`, `+triticale` with cover crop), `crops`,
`cover` (`triticale` or empty), `n_start_years`, the raw scores
above, `water_score`, `risk_score`, `soil_health_score`, info columns per pass (`window_days`, `cycle_days`, `irrigation_mm`,
`n_fertilizer_kg`, `cover_days`, `cover_eta_mm` (water the cover crop took), `frost_days`, `heat_days`, `kill_days`); `top_rotations.csv` adds `rank`, `total_score`, `is_current`.
`cycle_days` = first sowing to the first sowing of the next pass (the rotation repeating, incl. the gap after its last
crop, which the window leaves out). Per year of the rotation = pass value / (`cycle_days` / 365.25), e.g. continuous
corn: one season per year. Per window day × 365.25 overstates it by the left-out gap (up to ~25 %).

`rotation_seasons_by_year.csv`: `rotation`, `season` (0..2), `crop`, `start_year` (run), `sow_date`, `harvest_date`
(harvest day not part of the season), `harvest_year`, `season_days`, `etc_mm` (water the crop wants), `eta_mm`
(water it gets from rain and soil, rain-fed), `precipitation_mm`, `irrigation_need_mm` (= `etc_mm − eta_mm`),
`risk_per_crop_day`, `frost_days`, `heat_days`, `kill_days`, `n_fertilizer_kg_ha` (K-State, same every year),
`n_adjustment_kg_ha` (the formula's previous crop adjustment, negative = credit, e.g. after soybean, plus 27.5 lb N/ac
after a cover crop; before the K-State minimum / maximum; 0 for soybean), `cover`, `cover_sow_date`, `cover_end_date` (termination day), `cover_days`, `cover_eta_mm`: cover crop in the gap
after this season (empty if none).
One row per season and weather year (32–34 per season);
the previous crop is the real one of the rotation. Spread over weather years: group by `rotation`, `season`.
Rain-fed water ratio for a yield model: `eta_mm / etc_mm`.

`rotation_monthly.csv`: `rotation`, `year_offset` (0 = year of the first sowing), `month` (1–12), `crop` (what stands
on most days: a main crop, `triticale` or `fallow`), means per weather year: `window_days`, `crop_days`, `cover_days`, `et_mm` (crop ETc; bare soil
evaporation on fallow days), `precipitation_mm`, `irrigation_need_mm`, `heat_days`, `frost_days`, `kill_days`;
`heat_year_share`, `frost_year_share`, `kill_year_share` = share of weather years with at least one such day in that
month (0–1). Only months inside the window; the first and last month are partial (`window_days`).
Note: `frost_days` / `heat_days` are stress days from `crop_risk_score` (Tmin below / Tmax above the crop's optimum),
not frost in the narrow sense, so their year shares are close to 1 in most crop months. Freezing damage is `kill_days`;
only winter wheat in its winter rest has a documented killing temperature (see `crop_scorer/risk_score/README.md`).

`top_rotations_seasons.csv`: `rank`, `rotation`, `season` (0..2), `crop`, `sow_month_day`, `harvest_month_day`
(harvest day not part of the season), `sow_cycle_day`, `season_days`, `irrigation_need_mm`, `risk_per_crop_day`,
`frost_days`, `heat_days`, `kill_days`, `n_fertilizer_kg_ha` (means over the start years).

`top_rotations_daily_long.csv`: `rank`, `rotation`, `cycle_day` (0 = first sowing), `month_day`, `crop`
(or `fallow`), `parameter`, `value`, `unit`. Fallow days have no risk rows.

| parameter | unit | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `crop_evapotranspiration` | mm/day | water the crop wants (ETc) |
| `precipitation` | mm/day | rain |
| `irrigation_need` | mm/day | water the crop is missing (ETc − ETa) |
| `temperature_risk_score` | score | daily temperature risk (kill day = 100) |
| `frost_day_share`, `heat_day_share`, `kill_day_share` | share of years | how often this day is a frost / heat stress / cold-kill day |

Pivot: `df.pivot_table(index=["rank", "rotation", "cycle_day", "month_day", "crop"], columns="parameter", values="value")`.

## Known gaps / assumptions

- No penalty for the same crop in a row yet, so monocultures can rank high.
- Fallow loses `0.25 × ET0` from the whole bucket (water_usage assumption, calibrated to the 20–35 % fallow efficiency
  measured in western Kansas). The water cost of a cover crop is the difference to this fallow evaporation.
- Min-max depends on the set of rotations; a rotation outside it (other length) can score below 0 or above 1.
- Dates (`month_day`) come from the first start year; leap years can shift a day by one.
- Assumptions of the single scores apply (see `water_usage`, `crop_risk_score` and `erosion` READMEs),
  e.g. a shifted season keeps its length, so late double crops collect frost days while drying down.
